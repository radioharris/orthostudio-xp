These terms cover the files OrthoStudio XP Live downloads from OrthoStudio XP's server: the mesh
of each region (`z_osxp_*`), its river masks, its overlays (`y_osxp_*`) and its night map
(`night_*`), here called "the scenery". OrthoStudio XP's program is free software under the GNU
GPL (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`); the scenery is not covered by the GPL.

Copyright (C) 2026 radioharris, for radioharris's work in the scenery. The parts that come from
others stay theirs; the sources to credit are below.

## What you may do

- Download the scenery with OrthoStudio XP Live and fly over it in X-Plane, on your own computers.
- Show it: screenshots, videos and streams of your flights.

## What needs radioharris's written agreement

- Passing the scenery on, whole or in part, as it is or changed: uploading it, sharing it,
  mirroring it, serving it to others.
- Putting it in another program, scenery package, streaming service or download.
