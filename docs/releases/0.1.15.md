# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.15 (beta)

What users wrote in about since 0.1.14: the map says more about what you are looking at, busy
map data servers no longer fail a build, and every error says what to do.

## What changed

- **The map says what the view is worth**: the legend reads *Standard, about 2 m per pixel ·
  ZL16*, and past a source's deepest level it says the map is only enlarging.
- **The map zooms to ZL19**, the deepest level a tile is built at, and no deeper.
- **A yellow line says when the source has nothing where you look**: a country's source outside
  its country, or one with no photo this deep here.
- **Tiles built but not in X-Plane are on the map**, dashed pink, with a box in the legend to
  hide them.
- **Each square is framed inside its own edges**, so neighbours in different states keep their
  own colours.
- **The legend lines up, and folds away.**
- **The map stays where you left it** when you come back from Works: every build moved it in
  0.1.14.
- **A finished build leaves the Works list on its own**, with the bin on its row, and the next
  one takes its place on screen.
- **A source of your own is asked again after you change its address**, instead of reusing the
  first address's images.
- **Settings gives every number its range and its default**, first and in bold, and the colour
  previews are larger.
- **Every error says what to do**, not only what went wrong, and 59 more are in French.
- **A busy map data server is waited for**, instead of failing the build within a minute, and
  asked again when it says it will have room.
- **Imagery servers are approached at the rate they answer**, instead of held at one they may
  refuse, and EOX says the year of its imagery.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.15-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.15-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.15-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.15-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app,
and a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway. The known
limitations of 0.1.0 still apply:
[docs/releases/0.1.0.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.0.md).

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)). Its form asks for
the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile (`+46+006`), a screenshot, and
the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
