# OrthoStudio XP 2.0.0

OrthoStudio XP Live: aerial imagery under your aircraft wherever you fly, downloaded while you fly,
as XPME and AutoOrtho do, with no tiles to build first.

## What changed

- OrthoStudio XP Live: aerial imagery under your aircraft wherever you fly, downloaded while you fly, with no tiles to build first. Its guide: [docs/live.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/live.md).
- No virtual disk: no FUSE or WinFsp to install, nothing mounted. Live builds the textures beside X-Plane as ordinary files. X-Plane waits only while a flight or a new location loads, never while you fly, and flies on if Live stops.
- Measured on the same flight: the ground ready about twice as fast as with XPME and five times as fast as with AutoOrtho, for a third of a processor core ([the comparison](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/live.md#compared-with-xpme-and-autoortho)).
- A new location chosen in flight waits too, with any X-Plane 12: X-Plane pauses until the ground there is built.
- Prepare my departure before starting the flight: Live builds the ground around it before X-Plane loads the flight, which then starts at once, and its map goes there.
- Live brings its own mesh of the world, Antarctica aside, made by OrthoStudio XP's engine and installed by region from its Settings. Quit XPME or AutoOrtho before you start Live: its window checks that no other program streams ortho.
- Your own tiles come first: Live downloads nothing where you built one.
- With your SimBrief name, Live follows your flight plan and offers its departure to prepare.
- Seasons: the photos in the colours of spring, summer, autumn or winter, following the date of your flight in X-Plane or the one you choose, each with its sliders.
- One window for both: Build | Live at the top, the language, the theme, the text size and the zoom the same for both. A build while Live streams, or a flight while a build runs, asks first.
- At its first start, the app asks: build your tiles, or fly now with Live.
- Live is on by default: `enabled = false` in `~/.orthostudio/live.toml` switches it off.
- Live has flown with X-Plane 12 on a Mac and on a Windows PC, not yet on Linux. With less than 32 GB of memory, keep its default zoom.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the window, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself, Live included, and asks first if X-Plane is flying with Live.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 15 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.0.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.0.0-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.0.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.0.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed**, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app, and
a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway.

## Known limitations

Those of [1.0](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.0.0.md#known-limitations) and [1.4](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.4.0.md#known-limitations) still apply. Live's own:

- **Linux.** Live has flown with X-Plane 12 on Macs and on a Windows PC, not yet on Linux.
- **The mesh.** Live lays its photos on it, region by region (4 to 42 GB each, installed from its
  Settings): where no region is installed, X-Plane shows its own scenery, without photos.
- **One ortho program at a time.** Quit XPME or AutoOrtho before you start Live.
- **The window over a paused X-Plane** is not there on Linux: X-Plane waits, without Live's progress.
- **The disk.** Live writes more to the disk than XPME on the same flight: each texture is written
  whole as it gets sharper.
- **External views** far from the aircraft (more than 10 km) may show a green-grey ground until
  X-Plane loads that area again.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux). For Live, add
`LogLive.txt`, at the top of your X-Plane folder beside X-Plane's own `Log.txt`, and that `Log.txt`.
An idea goes to the [Ideas](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/ideas)
of the Discussions, where others can vote for it, and a question to
[Q&A](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/q-a).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
