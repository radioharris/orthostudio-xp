# OrthoStudio XP 2.3.0

## What changed

- *Install and relaunch*: the banner of a new version installs it and opens the app again in it, on macOS, Windows and Linux. It works from this version on, for the versions after it.
- A new coast choice, experimental: *Like a sandy beach, but rivers keep their water*. Narrow rivers keep their water in the middle, and both banks fade alike.
- The water masks count the memory they really take: with the inland water, the three steps or the new coast choice, fewer are made at once, so a 16 GB computer no longer runs short. Those tiles take longer; the default choice is unchanged.
- Fixed in Live: X-Plane cancelled the squares on the line between two of its regions, a `_water_overlay.ter` missing ([issue 36](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/36)).
- Fixed in Live: a damaged record of its textures kept it from starting, its window saying "HTTP 500" ([discussion 32](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/32)).
- Fixed in Live: X-Plane said some scenery textures could not be loaded, a night texture missing ([discussion 37](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/37)). A night texture that cannot be made now leaves its square without lights, and the start no longer waits for it.
- Fixed in Live: a square partly taken away had files missing when the aircraft came back.
- Live says that another tool's mesh has all its street lamps on at night, in the country too.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the window, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself, Live included, and asks first if X-Plane is flying with Live.
From 2.3.0 on, the banner of the next versions does it for you (*Install and relaunch*).

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 15 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.3.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.3.0-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.3.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-2.3.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed**, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app, and
a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway.

## Known limitations

Those of [1.0](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.0.0.md#known-limitations) and [1.4](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/1.4.0.md#known-limitations) still apply. Live's own:

- **Linux.** Live has flown with X-Plane 12 on Macs and on a Windows PC, not yet on Linux.
  *Install and relaunch* has been tried on Macs and on a Windows PC, on Linux only in a test
  container.
- **The mesh.** Live lays its photos on it, region by region (4 to 42 GB each, installed from its
  Settings), or on another tool's mesh: where there is none, X-Plane shows its own scenery,
  without photos.
- **One ortho program at a time.** Quit XPME or AutoOrtho before you start Live.
- **The disk.** Live writes more to the disk than XPME on the same flight: each texture is written
  whole as it gets sharper.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux). For Live, add
`LogLive.txt`, at the top of your X-Plane folder beside X-Plane's own `Log.txt`, and that `Log.txt`.
An idea goes to the [Ideas](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/ideas)
of the Discussions, where others can vote for it, and a question to
[Q&A](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/q-a).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
