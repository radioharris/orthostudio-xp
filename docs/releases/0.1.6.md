# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.6 (beta)

One fix, for everyone who flies above 50° of latitude: the Copernicus relief, and Canada's lidar
with it, could not be used there at all.

## What changed

- **The Copernicus relief north of 50° (and south of it).** Copernicus GLO-30 keeps about 30 m on
  the ground rather than one arc-second, so from 50° of latitude its cells carry 2400 columns
  instead of 3600, then fewer still further on. OrthoStudio XP expected a square and called those
  cells unreadable: the tile was **refused**, with a message that sent you to the X-Plane
  installer. Canada's lidar failed with it, since it is laid over Copernicus. Everything from
  Edmonton, Calgary and Vancouver northwards, Scotland, Scandinavia, the Baltic countries, Russia
  and Alaska, and the same band in the far south, was concerned. The cells are now read as they
  come: a tile over Edmonton builds, and its model gives 714 m at Edmonton International against
  723 m published, 667 m at Edmonton City Centre against 671 m. Nothing needs downloading again:
  the files already on your disk are good.
- **A relief that cannot be downloaded says so.** The message named no source and no reason, and
  told you to install a region of X-Plane you already had. It now says which relief failed and
  what the service answered, and offers the relief of X-Plane 12, which downloads nothing.

Reported twice by a user in Alberta, whose second report carried the words that found it. Thank
you.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit*, at the top right of its page), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops
a running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.6-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.6-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.6-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.6-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app;
the README says how to open it anyway. The known limitations of 0.1.0 still apply:
[docs/releases/0.1.0.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.0.md).

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)). Its form asks for
the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile (`+46+006`), a screenshot, and
the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.local/state/OrthoStudio XP/log` on Linux).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
