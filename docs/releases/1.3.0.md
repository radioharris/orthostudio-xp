# OrthoStudio XP 1.3.0

Faster builds that take far less memory, above all around big cities and on computers of 16 GB.

## What changed

**Builds**
- A tile's images start as soon as it and its neighbours are meshed, no longer after the whole batch's terrain.
- Each tile's tracing runs in a program of its own, which gives its memory back when it is done: a long batch no longer grows in memory from tile to tile.
- Each step waits for the memory it really takes, counted before it starts, instead of one figure for every tile: a computer of 16 GB builds big cities that ran it out of memory.
- With the road points limit raised above 200 000 (Settings, for experts, Roads), a big city's roads are traced in pieces on several cores: Atlanta at the service roads in a minute and a half instead of 23.
- The images download in a program of their own, at the line's full speed while the terrain is built, and a slow line no longer asks for most images twice.
- EOX is asked for 50 images a second at most, under the limit past which it refused images and the download fell by half.
- A cancelled build keeps the image pieces it received, even on a slow disk, and the next build does not download them again.

**New**
- *Memory for building* (Settings, for experts, This app): a build may take 80, 70 (the default) or 60 % of the memory, never more than what the other apps leave free.

**Ground** (build a tile again to get these)
- The airports of an EOX tile stay at ZL14, EOX's highest level, instead of its image enlarged to ZL18: the same detail, minutes less a tile.
- With the road points limit raised above 200 000, a tile is traced again once, in pieces: its flattened roads move by half a metre, 2 m at a few points.

**Interface**
- The Plan's download estimate counts the images by their source and level, and the relief it will download.
- A new zone *Same as the tiles* no longer offers a level above the tiles' source (above ZL14 over EOX), which the build refused.
- In French, a number no longer breaks across two lines.

## Measured: 1.2.1 against 1.3.0

Twelve tiles in one build, Bing at ZL16, roads flattened down to the service roads, from an empty
data folder, timed until the terrain of the twelve tiles was done (the images go on after):

| Twelve tiles, terrain done | 1.2.1 | 1.3.0 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mac, 48 GB, around Atlanta | 38.0 min | **8.8 min** |
| Mac, 48 GB, Brittany | 8.3 min | **3.8 min** |
| Windows PC, 16 GB, around Atlanta | out of memory after 4.7 min | **31.6 min** |
| Windows PC, 16 GB, Brittany | 25.7 min | **11.6 min** |

Around Atlanta on the Mac, the first images started after 5.8 min instead of 35.6, and the build
held 19.4 GB of memory at most instead of 41.6. At the default settings, with fewer roads, the gain
is smaller. Every step and the conditions:
[build times of 1.3](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/v1.3.0/docs/benchmarks/build-times-1.3.md).

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.3.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.3.0-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.3.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.3.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed**, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app, and
a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway.

## Known limitations

- **Older versions.** Once *Disk space* has deleted the data kept for older versions, an older
  version refuses the data folder; the README says how to go back.
- **No automatic update.** The page says when a new version is out and links to it; install it
  over the old one, as above.
- **X-Plane closed.** X-Plane reads new scenery when it starts: install or remove tiles while it is
  closed (OrthoStudio XP refuses while it runs), then start it.
- **One tile's imagery at a time.** Out of politeness towards the providers, the imagery of one
  tile downloads at a time.
- **Disk space.** A ZL16 tile takes about 1.5 to 4 GB with its cache, more with sharp airports and
  zones. The Plan says about how much before you build, Works' report what was really written.
- **The data folder's disk** must be formatted APFS, Mac OS Extended, NTFS or ext4: exFAT, FAT32
  and folders shared over a network are refused.
- **Unplugging the data folder's disk** while OrthoStudio XP reads it (a build, *Free space*) can
  close OrthoStudio XP at once: plug the disk back and open it again, nothing is lost.
- **Zones follow squares of about 1 km.** A zone sharpens every square of about 1 km it touches, so
  its sharper area runs in straight lines just outside the outline you drew; the Plan's map shows
  those squares.
- **Aerodromes by the sea.** Where a dried aerodrome meets the sea, its edge can stand a few metres
  above the water (6 m at Avranches, LFRW).
- **Linux is experimental.** Its installer is built and checked at every release, but it is
  downloaded far less often than the others.
- **For your own use.** The imagery and the map data come from public services whose terms apply to
  you: do not share or sell the tiles you build.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
An idea goes to the [Ideas](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/ideas)
of the Discussions, where others can vote for it, and a question to
[Q&A](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/q-a).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
