# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.0 (beta)

Orthophoto scenery for X-Plane 12: aerial imagery laid on the real relief, tile by tile, built
from a page that says in plain words what it is doing. A new program built on the rules of
Ortho4XP, by Oscar Pilote and its contributors, with a different architecture that builds tiles
faster (measured in the README).

This is a **beta**: please read the known limitations below, and tell us what you find.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

Each installer has its own Python inside: nothing else to install. The installers are **not
signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app; the README says how to
open it anyway. X-Plane 12 must be installed on the same computer.

## What you can do

- Pick tiles on a map, or around an airport, and draw sharper zones where you fly low.
- See the disk space, the download and the time before anything is built.
- Build and install in one click, and follow every step of every tile, with the time left and the
  download rate.
- Answer settings in plain words, with three presets; every Ortho4XP setting is still there for
  experts.
- Keep your tiles in a Library: remove them from X-Plane, add them back, delete them, free the
  cache. Tiles built by Ortho4XP can be imported and managed too.
- Keep the tiles, the cache and the downloaded imagery on an external disk, chosen in Settings.
- The relief comes from X-Plane 12 itself, and X-Plane's roads, forests and buildings stay on top of
  the photos (or none, for simHeaven X-World users).

## Known limitations

- **Beta quality.** Tiles have been checked in flight on a few regions only. Coasts, big lakes,
  mountains and airports elsewhere may show defects: please report them.
- **Windows**: tiles have been built and installed with the installer on Windows 11 on ARM, where
  the x64 app runs under emulation, more slowly; not yet on a PC with an Intel or AMD processor.
  No Developer Mode is needed: the tiles go into Custom Scenery as directory junctions.
- **Linux**: the installer is built and tested automatically, but nobody has built a tile with it
  yet; a first try on Linux will probably come later.
- **macOS**: Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later only.
- **No automatic update**: install the new version over the old one. On Windows the installer
  stops a running OrthoStudio XP first; on macOS, quit it (*Quit*, at the top right of its page)
  before replacing the app.
- **X-Plane reads new scenery when it starts**: install or remove tiles while X-Plane is closed
  (OrthoStudio XP refuses while it runs), then start X-Plane.
- **Imagery downloads one tile at a time**, out of politeness towards the providers: about 40 to 60 s
  for a ZL16 tile on a fast line, longer on a slow one. The map data of a new tile takes 8 to 15 s
  more when the public Overpass servers answer.
- **Disk space**: about 1.5 to 4 GB for a ZL16 tile, more with sharp airports and zones. The estimate
  says how much before you build.
- **Another data folder** takes the next downloads and builds, nothing already there is moved, and
  its disk must be formatted APFS, Mac OS Extended, NTFS or ext4 (exFAT and FAT32 are refused).
- **For your own use**: the imagery and the map data come from public services whose terms apply to
  you; do not share or sell the tiles you build.

## Reporting a problem

Say what you did and what you saw, and join:

- the tile (`+46+006`) and, for a visual defect, a screenshot with the position (latitude,
  longitude) or the nearest airport;
- your settings (Settings: the preset, or the answers you changed);
- the log `serve.log`: `~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.local/state/OrthoStudio XP/log` on Linux.
