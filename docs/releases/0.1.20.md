# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.20 (beta)

Your own reliefs at their full resolution, Works bars that move with the work, and a window that
opens on Windows even with an old WebView2.

## What changed

- Your own elevation files are read at their own resolution whatever their size, a 4 m lidar square included, as far as the computer's memory goes; a file too big for it is refused with the numbers.
- Works says when a file of your relief folder could not be used or was laid on a coarser grid, and when a tile's mesh reached its budget of triangles.
- Every Works bar moves with the work, even in the steps that count nothing (the mesh, the masks, the X-Plane terrain), by the time they usually take on your computer.
- What a step is doing, the speed of a download included, is in its tooltip; its cell says how far it is.
- On Windows, a WebView2 too old for the window (before version 101, of 2022) opens OrthoStudio XP in the browser, and the installer offers its update.
- On Windows, `serve.log` stays in UTF-8, and the lines the window writes there are no longer overwritten.
- A silent removal on Windows (`/VERYSILENT`) no longer waits on a message box.
- In Settings, the colour preview no longer makes the page jump, and changes at once with the source or the colours.
- *Default values* in Settings keeps where the map data comes from: your own folder and the map library.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.20-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.20-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.20-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.20-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app,
and a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway. The known
limitations of 0.1.0 still apply:
[docs/releases/0.1.0.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.0.md).

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)). Its form asks for
the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile (`+46+006`), a screenshot, and
the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
