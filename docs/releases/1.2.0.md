# OrthoStudio XP 1.2.0

A Disk space tab, roads without bumps, and tiles brought back into the workshop.

## What changed

**New**
- Disk space: a tab of its own, which shows what the data folder holds and says, box by box, what freeing each part changes and what would be downloaded again.
- Disk space: two new choices, the data kept for older versions (about 150 MB a tile), and the textures kept a second time of the tiles on another disk, which a rebuild makes again from the downloaded images.
- Library: *File elsewhere…* brings a tile back into the workshop when you choose its *Tiles* folder.
- Free space makes the photos a tile of the workshop holds twice one file again (a tile brought back from another disk), and a build no longer leaves them twice.
- Library: tiles OrthoStudio XP built that the Library lost come back, those of the workshop by themselves, the others with *Find tiles in a folder…*.
- Plan: squares of open sea are left out and said, where they were refused.

**Ground** (build a tile again to get these)
- Roads and airport edges get the rounded ends of Ortho4XP 1.30: no more bumps where roads meet (KSME, Kentucky).
- An older kind of Global Scenery file, whose sea-depth map has another size, is read instead of failing the build (Libya).
- The first build again after the update makes the terrain again once, about 20 s a tile on a recent Mac; the images are reused.

**Builds**
- When EOX refuses under load, the download pauses, slows down and asks again, instead of leaving textures missing.
- After an update, Build again's estimate counts the steps that changed at their usual time, not one second.
- Step 3 of the Plan counts the images to prepare apart from those already prepared.
- The terrain step says what it does at each airport, and a step far past its usual time says so.
- Works writes every level of a build: "ZL16 (2 tiles at ZL13)".

**Reliability**
- A disk unplugged during a build: the build says only that the data folder went, and the data folder is read again as soon as the disk is back, without a restart.
- A damaged data folder is said as such, with what to do.
- The checks' banner says each failed check in plain words, and follows the data folder when it comes and goes.
- On Windows, a data folder refused at the opening can be emptied while OrthoStudio XP runs.

**Interface**
- Disk space says *Measuring…* with a spinner during its first measure, and measures in about half the time.
- The help under the zone buttons follows a language switch.
- The advice for relief voids set to 0 m says when no setting fills them.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.0-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed**, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app, and
a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway.

## Known limitations

- **Older versions.** Once *Disk space* has deleted the data kept for older versions, an older
  version refuses the data folder; the README says how to go back.
- **No automatic update.** The page says when a new version is out and links to it; install it
  over the old one, as above.
- **X-Plane closed.** X-Plane reads new scenery when it starts: install or remove tiles while it is
  closed (OrthoStudio XP refuses while it runs), then start it.
- **One tile's imagery at a time.** Out of politeness towards the providers, the imagery of one
  tile downloads at a time.
- **Disk space.** A ZL16 tile takes about 1.5 to 4 GB with its cache, more with sharp airports and
  zones. The Plan says about how much before you build, Works' report what was really written.
- **The data folder's disk** must be formatted APFS, Mac OS Extended, NTFS or ext4: exFAT, FAT32
  and folders shared over a network are refused.
- **Zones follow squares of about 1 km.** A zone sharpens every square of about 1 km it touches, so
  its sharper area runs in straight lines just outside the outline you drew; the Plan's map shows
  those squares.
- **Aerodromes by the sea.** Where a dried aerodrome meets the sea, its edge can stand a few metres
  above the water (6 m at Avranches, LFRW).
- **Linux is experimental.** Its installer is built and checked at every release, but it is
  downloaded far less often than the others.
- **For your own use.** The imagery and the map data come from public services whose terms apply to
  you: do not share or sell the tiles you build.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
An idea goes to the [Ideas](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/ideas)
of the Discussions, where others can vote for it, and a question to
[Q&A](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/q-a).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
