# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.22 (beta)

OrthoStudio XP Live: aerial imagery under your aircraft wherever you fly, downloaded while you fly,
as AutoOrtho does, with no tiles to build first.

## What changed

- At its first start, the app asks: fly now with OrthoStudio XP Live, or build your tiles. Each window has a button to the other.
- Live downloads the imagery while you fly, as AutoOrtho does, with no tile to build first; X-Plane never waits for it in flight.
- At a flight's start, X-Plane waits on its loading screen until the ortho around the aircraft is built.
- Live lays its photos on AutoOrtho's mesh, installed by region from its window. Quit AutoOrtho before you start Live.
- Your own tiles come first: Live downloads nothing where you built one.
- With your SimBrief name, Live follows your flight plan and can build the ortho round your departure before X-Plane starts.
- No zoom by flight level, prefetch or waiting settings: [why, and what replaces each AutoOrtho setting](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/live.md#why-there-are-no-zoom-prefetch-or-waiting-settings).
- Live is on in this beta: `enabled = false` in `~/.orthostudio/live.toml` switches it off.
- Live has flown with X-Plane 12 on a Mac and on a Windows PC, not yet on Linux. With 16 GB of memory, keep its default zoom.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself, Live included, and asks first if X-Plane is flying with Live.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app,
and a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway. The known
limitations of 0.1.0 still apply:
[docs/releases/0.1.0.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.0.md).

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)). Its form asks for
the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile (`+46+006`), a screenshot, and
the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux). For Live, add
`LogLive.txt`, at the top of your X-Plane folder beside X-Plane's own `Log.txt`, and that `Log.txt`.
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
