# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.22 (beta)

Build your tiles in one place, then keep them wherever you like: the Library files them on any
disk, and X-Plane follows them. How it works:
[docs/workshop.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/workshop.md).

## What changed

- *File elsewhere…* in the Library files the tiles you tick into any folder, on any disk: moved at once on the same disk, copied and read back on another. X-Plane and the Library follow them, roads and forests included, and a tile turned off in X-Plane stays off.
- A filed tile built again is built in the workshop, then put back in its folder; built unchanged, it copies nothing.
- *Free space…* can free the cache kept for the tiles filed elsewhere, on a line of its own; the tiles stay complete and in X-Plane.
- A tile whose folder you moved yourself says *Not found*, with *Find again…*: show where you put it, and the Library and X-Plane follow it.
- A tile on a disk that is not plugged in says *Disk not connected*, and comes back by itself once the disk is plugged in.
- When your tiles are in more than one folder, the Library writes the folder under each tile, and a *Folder* list beside the search shows one folder's tiles.
- The Library's tick boxes have a narrow column of their own, so the tile names line up.
- Each screen keeps its own scroll position, and a new build opens Works at its top.
- A tile X-Plane shows is never rewritten while X-Plane runs: quit X-Plane, then build it again.
- The Library names your own elevation file a tile stands on, or says why it was not used.
- A data folder reached through a link no longer gives a tile two rows in the Library.
- `− 100 % +` at the foot of the window zooms the whole page (Ctrl or ⌘ with plus, minus and 0 too), and `Aa` beside it makes the text alone larger.
- The window opens where you left it, the size it had.
- On Windows, downloads no longer all fail at once when the name of your user folder has an accented letter.
- On Windows, the installer opens the program once before its first start, so that the antivirus has read its files by then.
- When an imagery server asks for a pause, nothing at all is sent to it until the pause is over.
- The Library's Disk space lines follow a change of language.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.22-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app,
and a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway. The known
limitations of 0.1.0 still apply:
[docs/releases/0.1.0.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.0.md).

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
Something you would like OrthoStudio XP to do has its own form, *A request*, and a question goes to
the [Discussions](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
