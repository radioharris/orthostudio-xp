# OrthoStudio XP 1.2.1

Big cities at high road levels trace as fast as in 1.1.0 again, and the roads can be smoothed.

## What changed

**Ground** (build a tile again to get these)
- The ends of the flattened roads are angular again by default, as in 1.1.0: a big city at the service roads traces in half the time and memory, and tiles built with 1.1.0 keep their terrain.
- *Rounded road ends* (Settings, for experts, Roads) brings back the rounded ends of 1.2.0, which take the bumps out of crossroads; tiles built with 1.2.0 keep their terrain with it ticked.
- *Smooth the roads* (Settings, for experts, Roads) evens the flattened roads over 100 or 250 m, so that X-Plane's roads no longer bump over the small waves of the relief. Off by default.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.1-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.1-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.1-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.2.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed**, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app, and
a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway.

## Known limitations

- **Older versions.** Once *Disk space* has deleted the data kept for older versions, an older
  version refuses the data folder; the README says how to go back.
- **No automatic update.** The page says when a new version is out and links to it; install it
  over the old one, as above.
- **X-Plane closed.** X-Plane reads new scenery when it starts: install or remove tiles while it is
  closed (OrthoStudio XP refuses while it runs), then start it.
- **One tile's imagery at a time.** Out of politeness towards the providers, the imagery of one
  tile downloads at a time.
- **Disk space.** A ZL16 tile takes about 1.5 to 4 GB with its cache, more with sharp airports and
  zones. The Plan says about how much before you build, Works' report what was really written.
- **The data folder's disk** must be formatted APFS, Mac OS Extended, NTFS or ext4: exFAT, FAT32
  and folders shared over a network are refused.
- **Unplugging the data folder's disk** while OrthoStudio XP reads it (a build, *Free space*) can
  close OrthoStudio XP at once: plug the disk back and open it again, nothing is lost.
- **Zones follow squares of about 1 km.** A zone sharpens every square of about 1 km it touches, so
  its sharper area runs in straight lines just outside the outline you drew; the Plan's map shows
  those squares.
- **Aerodromes by the sea.** Where a dried aerodrome meets the sea, its edge can stand a few metres
  above the water (6 m at Avranches, LFRW).
- **Linux is experimental.** Its installer is built and checked at every release, but it is
  downloaded far less often than the others.
- **For your own use.** The imagery and the map data come from public services whose terms apply to
  you: do not share or sell the tiles you build.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
An idea goes to the [Ideas](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/ideas)
of the Discussions, where others can vote for it, and a question to
[Q&A](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/q-a).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
