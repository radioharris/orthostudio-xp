# OrthoStudio XP 1.1.0

Build again from the Library, Little Navmap flight plans, and drier ground at airports by the water.

## What changed

**New**
- Library: *Build again…* builds the ticked tiles again with today's version and settings, without the Plan.
- Library: every action of a row works on all the ticked tiles at once, Ortho4XP tiles included; *Show* keeps to tiles with files missing or squares listed twice.
- Plan: *Open a flight plan…* takes the squares along a Little Navmap plan (.lnmpln) or an X-Plane .fms, as with SimBrief.
- Plan: the map shows the squares of about 1 km each zone will take, and the list says when a zone takes none.
- Settings: a third overlay answer, X-Plane's roads, railways and power lines only, for a pack that brings its own forests and buildings.
- Settings: your own elevation files can be used first, even where they are coarser than the relief chosen.
- Settings: untick *Flatten OpenStreetMap's helipads* to keep a helipad on its slope.
- Settings: limit_tris goes up to just under 50 million triangles, as in Ortho4XP.
- Works: the final report shows what each step really wrote to disk and downloaded.

**Ground** (build a tile again to get these)
- The water an aerodrome's runways, taxiways or aprons touch becomes land, as in XPME: no more taxiway bridges under water at Tokyo Haneda. Seaplane bases keep their water.
- Rainwater basins that OpenStreetMap marks as dry most of the year stay ground instead of water.
- Tokyo (+35+139) no longer comes out with a coarse mesh, its mountains under triangles a kilometre long.
- A coastline drawn on land (a market in Tehran, a hotel in Manaus) no longer turns a whole tile into sea.
- No more dark blue triangles of deep water along some straight shores (Groom Lake).

**Plan and Build again**
- A build's cost comes from what the data folder already holds and from each tile's last build: after an update, Build again no longer counts cached images as new.
- A disk that may run short is said in amber and no longer blocks a build: if it fills up, the build stops cleanly and resumes later.
- The Plan counts the photos of sharper airports for tiles not built yet, and works without X-Plane's airport list.
- A spinner shows while the Library works out the cost of Build again.

**Reliability**
- A build cut short when the program stopped is listed in Works as interrupted, with Build again; the next start says it is checking what that build left.
- The engine's helper processes stop with it, instead of keeping memory and the data disk busy until a restart.
- The map library's connections recover at once after a network or VPN change.
- Builds with USGS 1/3″ or ANADEM relief count the memory that step really takes, so fewer run side by side.

**Interface**
- Settings changed and not saved show until they are saved: a dot on the Settings tab, a notice in the Plan.
- The Library's error cards show above the table, its actions sit in three groups, and a click on a tile's name ticks it.
- The import button reads *Import tiles built elsewhere…*: it takes photo tiles whatever made them.
- Works' final report counts the textures of every level, sharper airports and zones included.
- A zone that only recolours no longer draws an area around it, and the zone help is shorter.
- Settings' refusals name the field and the values it takes.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit* in the page, or Cmd+Q), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops a
running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.1.0-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.1.0-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.1.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-1.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed**, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app, and
a browser may refuse to download them; the README says how to open them anyway.

## Known limitations

- **No automatic update.** The page says when a new version is out and links to it; install it
  over the old one, as above.
- **X-Plane closed.** X-Plane reads new scenery when it starts: install or remove tiles while it is
  closed (OrthoStudio XP refuses while it runs), then start it.
- **One tile's imagery at a time.** Out of politeness towards the providers, the imagery of one
  tile downloads at a time.
- **Disk space.** A ZL16 tile takes about 1.5 to 4 GB with its cache, more with sharp airports and
  zones. The Plan says about how much before you build (it counts too much over open sea), Works'
  report what was really written.
- **The data folder's disk** must be formatted APFS, Mac OS Extended, NTFS or ext4: exFAT, FAT32
  and folders shared over a network are refused.
- **Zones follow squares of about 1 km.** A zone sharpens every square of about 1 km it touches, so
  its sharper area runs in straight lines just outside the outline you drew; the Plan's map shows
  those squares.
- **Aerodromes by the sea.** Where a dried aerodrome meets the sea, its edge can stand a few metres
  above the water (6 m at Avranches, LFRW).
- **Linux is experimental.** Its installer is built and checked at every release, but it is
  downloaded far less often than the others.
- **For your own use.** The imagery and the map data come from public services whose terms apply to
  you: do not share or sell the tiles you build.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)) and choose
*A problem*. Its form asks for the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile
(`+46+006`), a screenshot, and the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.orthostudio/log` on Linux).
An idea goes to the [Ideas](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/ideas)
of the Discussions, where others can vote for it, and a question to
[Q&A](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/discussions/categories/q-a).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
