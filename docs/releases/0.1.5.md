# OrthoStudio XP 0.1.5 (beta)

A street map and the airports on the Plan's map, the downloaded relief freed like the rest, and the
answer to a report from Alberta: a relief service that stays silent is no longer remembered as a
region without lidar.

## What changed

- **The street map, in one click.** The legend of the Plan's map has a box that swaps the aerial
  photo for OpenStreetMap: towns, roads and names, to see what a square holds before choosing it.
  A user of the X-Plane.Org page asked for it, as Ortho4XP offers it in its preview window. The map
  comes from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org), which renders OpenStreetMap for free and
  without a key; OrthoStudio XP reads it through its own engine, so the page still talks to nothing
  but the address it was opened at, and what is downloaded is kept in the map cache. The renderer
  it needs travels with the app and is loaded the first time you ask for the street map, not
  before.
- **The airports, with their ICAO code.** A second box draws the aerodromes of the view over the
  photo or the street map, from zoom 8 in, with their code from zoom 9. It answers the question the
  aerial imagery often does not: is the field I want inside this square? Only the fields that have
  a real code are drawn, since X-Plane's own identifiers say nothing to a pilot; the codes never
  sit on each other, nor on their own point. It costs no download: the list travels with the app,
  built from the airports of your X-Plane.
- **The downloaded relief, counted and freed.** The elevation cells downloaded from Copernicus,
  the USGS or Canada's lidar were kept for ever: 1.5 GB after a few tiles, which nothing could
  give back. The Library's disk box now shows them, with their own box to free them, and the
  terminal has `osxp clean --relief` (and `--all`, which includes them). They are still kept by
  default: a square comes back in seconds from the disk and in minutes from the network.
- **A relief service that does not answer is not a region without lidar.** Canada's lidar is
  asked first for a small probe, and a probe that times out was written down as "no lidar here"
  for thirty days -- so a user in Alberta was told his region had none, and the tile fell back to
  Copernicus for a month. Only a service that answers, and says it has nothing, is remembered now;
  silence and server errors are forgotten at once, and the next build asks again.
- **Folders written with `~`.** Where the page reads out a folder (the status bar, the Library's
  line about which X-Plane it speaks of, Settings' detected folder and data folder), what lies
  under your home folder is now written `~/X-Plane 12` rather than in full. It is shorter to read,
  and it keeps your name out of the pictures you post with a report.

## Install

Download the installer of your system below, then follow *Getting started* in the README.
Installed over an older version, it keeps your tiles and settings: on macOS, quit OrthoStudio XP
first (*Quit*, at the top right of its page), then replace the app; on Windows, the installer stops
a running OrthoStudio XP by itself.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.5-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 15 or later, Intel | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.5-macos-x86_64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.5-windows-x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux, 64-bit (experimental) | `OrthoStudio-XP-0.1.5-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` |

The installers are **not signed** yet, so macOS and Windows warn the first time you open the app;
the README says how to open it anyway. The known limitations of 0.1.0 still apply:
[docs/releases/0.1.0.md](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/blob/main/docs/releases/0.1.0.md).

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue in this repository
([New issue](https://github.com/radioharris/orthostudio-xp/issues/new/choose)). Its form asks for
the version, your system, what you did and what you saw, the tile (`+46+006`), a screenshot, and
the log `serve.log` (`~/Library/Logs/OrthoStudio XP` on macOS,
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\OrthoStudio XP\Logs` on Windows, `~/.local/state/OrthoStudio XP/log` on Linux).
Without a GitHub account, the comments on OrthoStudio XP's page on X-Plane.Org are read too.
